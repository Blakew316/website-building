What is Jungle Scout? Pricing, Reviews & Features for Sellers

If you're a small business owner, you've likely considered selling your products on Amazon. Amazon is the world's largest retailer and has an incredible program for resellers. If you plan on growing your presence on this website, you can maximize your profits and reach a larger number of customers using the right keywords and product strategy.

Jungle Scout is designed to help small business owners conduct product research and optimize their product listings on the Amazon marketplace. With their suite of innovative tools, data dashboards, and product features, businesses can develop profitable campaigns that deliver results.

Here's everything you need to know about the benefits of Jungle Scout for small business owners:

What is Jungle Scout?

Jungle Scout is an Amazon seller software tool that brands can use to drive growth and stay strategic on Amazon Marketplace. The platform helps brands build, manage, and maintain their Amazon business. There is also a suite of data tools to learn more about your competitors and find new product opportunities.

With the platform, small business owners can boost brand visibility and increase profits on Amazon. You can also analyze product trends, optimize your listings, and find new growth opportunities. With this suite of tools, you can ensure your brand gets noticed on Amazon while streamlining your day-to-day tasks.

7 Benefits of Using Jungle Scout for Small Business Owners

If you plan on selling on Amazon, you'll need reliable seller software to get strategic with your approach. The marketplace on Amazon is competitive. To ensure your product gets noticed by your target audience, you'll need a platform like Jungle Scout.

Here are seven benefits and Jungle Scout features you can look forward to:

1. Keyword Search

With Jungle Scout, you can search products by keyword. You can also save your keyword lists and utilize keyword discovery tools to unlock new opportunities. With these tools, you can optimize the language you use to market your products and enhance the listings, ensuring that your brand gets noticed by your target audience.

2. Free Learning Resources

Are you new to the world of selling on Amazon? Jungle Scout offers a variety of video guides, learning resources, and case studies you can use to expand your knowledge. You can use these tools to get more strategic with your marketing and product development approach.

3. Data Tools and Profitability Dashboards

Jungle Scout has a suite of data and analytic tools you can use to improve your approach to Amazon marketing. The platform also has historical data analysis, which you can use to better understand monthly sales, monthly revenue, and net profits for a specific item you have listed. You can use these tools and data dashboards to better understand product profitability potential and find future opportunities for growth.

4. Listing Optimization Tools

Are you struggling to increase traffic to certain listings? Jungle Scout will offer expert recommendations and help you find new ways to maximize your results. With this tool, you can get more strategic with the keywords you use for the title and description. Jungle Scout will also make image recommendations.

5. PPC Reporting

With the help of Jungle Scout's keyword research tools, you can develop innovative PPC campaigns that help you expand your reach and boost online visibility. PPC campaigns can help you maximize your revenue and be more competitive. This is a great marketing tactic to consider exploring if you're still in the early stages of growing your presence on this platform.

6. New Product Development

Ready to launch a new product? Are you on the hunt for lucrative niches and opportunities? With Jungle Scout's opportunity finder and browser extension, you can identify new product trends in real-time. You can also review up to two years of historical sales data for the product or niche.

7. Boost Sales Reviews

Customer reviews drive sales and build trust with new customers exploring your listings. Reviews are a key component for growth and can help you stay competitive.

Jungle Scout can help you request product reviews from past customers. After a purchase, Jungle Scout will send a review request. You can automate these sends so that you're constantly receiving a new stream of reviews. This is a great way to build your brand on Amazon while building trust with a new prospective audience.

Jungle Scout's Pricing

With Jungle Scout, you have the option to pay monthly or annually. The platform has a variety of pricing tiers available. When exploring the different options, it's best to consider your unique needs and the size of your business. Here's how Jungle Scout pricing compares monthly:

  • Starter - $49/month
  • Growth Accelerator - $79/month
  • Brand Owner + CI - $149/month
  • Enterprise Solutions - Custom pricing, call for more information

If you're ready to commit to a full year, you can save up to 40% by commiting to an annual plan.
