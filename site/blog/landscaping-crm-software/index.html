How Landscaping Companies Can Benefit From CRM Software

CCBy Charlie Company Editorial Team·Aug 22, 2024·5 min read

According to the 2023 Financial Benchmark Study, landscaping companies have a median of 325 customers. That's a lot of lawns to mow and hedges to trim! If you're running a landscaping business, you know how important those customers are. Their repeat business and referrals keep your company growing and thriving.

Yet, managing dozens or even hundreds of unique customer relationships can get complicated fast. Every customer is unique, with their own needs and preferences. They deserve personalized attention, but how can you manage that when juggling so many clients?

CRM software streamlines customer interactions in one easy-to-use system. In this article, we'll show you how the right CRM can help your landscaping business.

What is a CRM for Landscaping?

First things first – what exactly is CRM, and how does it work? CRM stands for customer relationship management. Let's walk through an example. Someone contacts your landscaping company about a potential project. They become a lead. If that lead ultimately hires you for services, they graduate to being a customer.

A CRM helps you manage both leads and customers in one place.

Think of it as a software platform that helps you keep track of all your customer information and interactions. For landscapers, this means storing details about what each client wants (like weekly lawn mowing or monthly hedge trimming), their contact info, service history, and any special requests (like using only organic fertilizers).

But a good lawn care CRM does more than just store information. It can help you schedule appointments, send reminders, and even handle billing. It's like having an extra set of hands to help run your business.

Benefits of CRMs for Landscaping Companies

Landscaping companies have done just fine for centuries without fancy software. So why turn to CRM now? Although traditional methods still work, CRMs can take your business to the next level.

Here are four compelling benefits:

Keep Track of New Landscaping Leads

New landscaping leads can easily fall through the cracks when you already have a full schedule of existing customers. But those leads could be your next big customers.

A landscaping CRM helps you keep all your leads in one place. It can even grab lead info from your email and website automatically. This way, you won't miss out on any potential business. You can easily see who's interested and follow up at the right time.

For example, someone fills out a form on your website asking about lawn care services. Your lawn care CRM can automatically create a new lead, reminding you to call them within 24 hours. No more lost sticky notes or forgotten callbacks.

Increase Recurring Business

It's not just about attracting new leads, either. Existing customers are valuable, too. In fact, repeat customers spend 67% more than one-time clients. Sure, some folks might only need you to install a new garden path once. But most customers will need ongoing services like regular lawn mowing or seasonal plantings.

Landscaping CRM software helps you stay engaged with existing customers and even anticipate their needs proactively. With task reminders and follow-ups, you can provide 5-star service that keeps loyal clients coming back. Access their data instantly to understand precisely what they want and when they want it.

Stay Organized

Our guess is you'd rather be out under the shining sun making yards beautiful than stuck doing paperwork. With features like task management and calendar integration, a CRM keeps your schedule and to-do list all in one place. You can easily see what needs to be done daily, prioritize your tasks, and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

Plus, many CRMs can send automatic confirmations and reminders to your clients.
