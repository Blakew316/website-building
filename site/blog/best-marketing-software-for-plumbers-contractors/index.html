Best Marketing Software for Plumbers & Contractors

CCBy Charlie Company Editorial Team·Aug 11, 2026·5 min read

You wouldn't show up to a job without the right tools.

No pipe wrench, no job. No multimeter, no job. Wrong tools make everything harder, slower, and more expensive.

Marketing works the same way.

If you're using the wrong tools to get customers, or five different tools that don't talk to each other, you're working twice as hard to get half the results. The right marketing software makes getting new customers easier. It keeps old ones coming back. And it does a lot of the work while you're out doing what you actually do.

This post breaks it all down. Plain and simple.

What Marketing Software Even Means

Marketing software is just a tool that helps people find your business and helps you stay in touch with them after they do.

That's it.

It might help you show up on Google. Or send a text to a past customer. Or collect reviews after a job. Or remind someone that you're still around when they need a plumber again.

Good marketing software doesn't replace you. It just makes sure you're not invisible.

Forbes Advisor's guide to the best plumbing software puts it well: plumbers and contractors who use the right business tools grow faster and spend less time chasing work. The tools do the heavy lifting. You do the plumbing.

What Plumbers and Contractors Actually Need From Marketing Software

Not all marketing tools are built for the way you work. Here's what to look for.

Something That Helps New Customers Find You

When a pipe bursts at 9 p.m., homeowners don't flip through a phone book. They search Google.

You need to show up. That means having a clean Google listing, a website that loads fast on a phone, and your name showing up in the right spots when people search for a plumber or contractor nearby.

Some marketing tools help you with this. Many don't. Make sure the one you pick does.

A Way to Stay in Touch With Past Customers

Your best customers are the ones you already worked with.

They trust you. They know you show up. They'll call you again if you give them a reason to remember you.

A good marketing tool sends them a message every now and then. A seasonal reminder. A quick check-in. A note that says you're still around. That's how you get repeat business without spending a dollar on ads.

Automated Review Requests

Reviews are free advertising.

A homeowner who's deciding between two plumbers will almost always pick the one with more Google reviews. But most customers won't leave one unless you ask.

The right software asks for you. It sends a short text or email right after a job. The customer taps a link and leaves a review in two minutes. You get a new five-star without picking up the phone.

Email and Text in One Place

Some tools only do email. Some only do texts. You want both.

Different customers respond to different things. Some check their email every morning. Some only respond to texts. A good marketing platform lets you reach both without logging into two separate apps.

Reporting That Makes Sense

You should know if your marketing is working.

Not in a complicated way. Just: are more people calling? Are past customers coming back? Are reviews going up?

Real plumbers and contractors on Reddit comparing their software options say the same thing over and over: they want simple reports they can actually understand. Not a dashboard that takes an hour to read.

How the Top Platforms Stack Up

Mailchimp is great for email. Easy to use. Affordable. But it only does email. No texting. No CRM. No Google reviews. No local SEO. You'd need other tools to cover everything else.

Jobber is solid for scheduling and invoicing. Some communication tools built in. Lighter on the marketing side. Good if your main problem is organizing jobs, not getting more of them.

Broadly is built for home service businesses. Handles reviews and messaging well. Doesn't do much on the marketing or lead generation side.

HubSpot is powerful but built for bigger companies with marketing teams. Too complex and too expensive for most plumbers and contractors.

ServiceTitan is the big one in the trades. Great for larger operations. Steep price. Steep learning curve.

Quora's community of contractors and home service owners keeps coming back to the same answer: the most effective marketing for plumbers combines a strong Google presence with consistent follow-up. The challenge is finding one tool that does both without needing a tech team to run it.

Most of the platforms above do one or two things well. The gap shows up when you want your marketing, your customer list, your reviews, and your local presence to all work together.

The Part That Changes Everything

Most marketing tools give you a place to send emails or track customers.

The ones that actually grow your business go a step further. They connect your marketing to your operations. So when a job closes, the follow-up message goes out automatically. When a review comes in, you see it right away. When a past customer's name pops up in your list, you already know what they had done and when.

That connection is what turns a good tool into a business asset.
