Carpenter Marketing: Top 25 Carpentry Business Marketing Ideas To Get More Carpentry Leads

KCBy Kate Curtis·May 17, 2022·14 min read

With the second quarter of 2022 in full swing, now is the perfect time to try some new carpentry business marketing ideas to grow your carpentry business. If you’ve been wondering how to get more carpentry leads, we’ve got a ton of things you can try—and it all begins with your carpentry website design.

It’s important to try a variety of things when it comes to carpenter marketing. Why? Because every carpentry provider needs carpentry leads to survive—no matter your location or how long you’ve been completing carpentry jobs for your community. Without a good flow of new carpentry leads, it becomes impossible to grow your carpentry business.

But, if you create a system that attracts new customers and delivers new carpentry leads to your business like clockwork, that’s a recipe for:

  • More high-paying carpentry jobs
  • Increased revenue for you and your crew
  • A dependable way to grow your carpentry business

We know why carpentry providers need to generate carpentry leads, but how can you do it affordably and efficiently? The answer is: a good carpentry website with good carpentry website design. If you’ve ever wondered how to get more carpentry leads, follow the steps below—and consider working with carpentry marketing companies to grow your carpentry business!

1. Get More Carpentry Leads Through Your Website

If you don’t yet have a carpentry website, one of the best things you can do for your business is to create one. A carpentry website acts as your business’s online storefront—it’s the cornerstone of your online presence and gives potential customers a place to:

  • Learn more about your carpentry business
  • Read about the carpentry services you provide
  • See your service area
  • Get in touch with you about carpentry jobs
  • Become a quality carpentry lead for your business

Without a carpentry website and professional carpentry website design, how are your potential customers going to find your business on Google? How will they know where to contact you? They won’t.

Without a website, your business might as well not exist at all. Carpentry marketing companies can help you get set up.

2. Use Pay-Per-Click (PPC) and Search Engine Marketing (SEM)

A good PPC and SEM campaign is one carpentry advertising idea that’s been proven time and again to generate quality carpentry leads and grow your carpentry business. So, if you’ve been wondering how to get more carpentry leads, give this a shot!

While Google is the most popular search engine for these types of campaigns, thanks to its widespread popularity, don’t sleep on Bing. PPC and SEM are great ways to bring in carpentry leads.

Still on the fence? Check out these stats:

  • For every $1 spent in Google Ads, businesses make an average of $2.
  • About 41% of clicks on a search results page go to one of the top 3 paid ads.
  • More than 85% of people use the internet to find local carpentry providers.
  • Visitors who come to your website via PPC or SEM are 50% more likely to make a purchase.

Sounds good, right? Reach out to us to learn more about how we can help you create a powerful PPC and SEM campaign that will help you with carpentry lead generation. Carpentry marketing companies can also set you up with a professional carpentry website design and share other ideas for carpentry marketing to help grow your carpentry business.

3. Always Use Local SEO to Generate Carpentry Leads

Search engine optimization (SEO for carpenters) is still king in the carpentry digital marketing space, which is why SEO for carpenters is so popular. SEO is what moves your carpentry business to the top of page 1 on Google and keeps it there. If your campaign is robust enough, SEO for carpenters alone can generate the majority of your carpentry leads (but we still recommend reading the rest of these carpentry business marketing ideas)!

A good first step is setting up your Google My Business account. From there, you can advance to backlinking and content marketing.

It’s always a good idea to work with carpentry marketing companies and let an expert in SEO for carpenters get you up and running.

4. Get More Carpentry Leads Through Facebook + Instagram

Still wondering how to get more carpentry leads? Advertising on Facebook and Instagram can be a cost-effective method for carpenter marketing, provided you put the right strategy in place and know how to navigate the ad builders on these platforms.

You can target your ideal carpentry customers by their location and other pages they’ve liked—such as your competitors’ pages.

Displaying ads to this audience has the added benefit of creating and boosting awareness of your business. If people see your logo and your ads all over the place, they’re more likely to remember you when they need carpentry providers in the future. If you’ve been wanting to try social media advertising for your business, this is one carpentry business marketing idea worth trying.

Paired with SEO for carpenters and professional carpentry website design, this is a powerful way to grow your carpentry business. Carpentry marketing companies can get you set up.

5. How to Get More Carpentry Leads Through Strategic Alliances

Another smart way to generate carpentry leads is to partner up with the suppliers in your area, such as hardware stores and big-box stores. That could be Lowe’s, Home Depot, or a mom-and-pop shop down the street. Creating a partnership means they’ll recommend you to their customers and provide a steady stream of carpentry leads.

6. Create a Carpentry Facebook Group

Having a dedicated Facebook business page for your carpentry business is a must, but creating a group page for your business is a good idea, too. Post helpful content (maybe something you wrote for content marketing!) and interact with group members to start building trust and brand awareness in your audience. Down the road, that trust could turn into carpentry leads and help grow your carpentry business.

7. Content Marketing = Carpenter Marketing

Creating content for your ideal customers is a great way to get more carpentry leads. Consider writing blog posts and articles that you can post on your carpentry website, and make it easy for your audience to share that content on social media.

Here are some topic ideas:

  • Woodworking for Beginners
  • Top Home Projects of 2022
  • 10 Must-Have Tools for Your Toolbelt

As you write these articles, plug your carpentry. Suggest that readers use a carpentry provider they can trust to do the job correctly the first time, and—if you’re affiliated with a certain carpentry brand—talk them up!

8. Create Helpful YouTube Videos About Carpentry

YouTube is a popular platform, so why not use it to your advantage? Make an account (it’s free!) and start posting helpful carpentry videos. The videos can document your day-to-day activities, follow a job from start to finish, or illustrate how to properly repair something. These videos will build your credibility as a carpentry business and make it easier for viewers to see that you know what you’re doing and build trust.

In the video description, include your contact information and a link to your carpentry website so they can learn more.

9. Email Marketing = Carpenter Marketing, Too!

Email marketing is one of the oldest forms of digital marketing, and there’s a reason it’s stuck around so long: it works. For every dollar you spend on email marketing for your carpentry business, you’ll make (on average) a $40 return on your investment.

Because most carpentry businesses overlook the benefits of email marketing, it’s a great lead generation option for your business. For instance, businesses that stay in touch with their ideal customers via email generate 50% more sales-ready carpentry leads and spend 33% less money on making sales.

10. How to Get More Carpentry Leads at Trade Shows

Attending trade shows should be high on your list of carpentry business marketing ideas. They not only provide plenty of solutions for how to get more carpentry leads, but also let you observe your competition, create new business partnerships and boost brand awareness among potential customers.

To make the most of your trade show experience, make sure your booth provides something valuable to your audience, such as a freebie or another incentive, like coupons, giveaways, contests and more.

Finally, make sure you’re collecting information from visitors and following up to help grow your carpentry business.

11. Get Carpentry Business Marketing Ideas From Your Chamber of Commerce

If you haven’t already joined your local chamber of commerce, attend their next meeting and start networking with other local business owners. In particular, try to connect with property managers, developers and carpentry contractors—they’re likely to send more carpentry leads your way!

12. Give Telemarketing a Chance to Generate Carpentry Leads

Though telemarketing was once a good channel for carpentry lead generation, many people now block unwanted calls. However, there are still companies out there that will telemarket for you and set up appointments with potential carpentry customers.

While this is a viable solution for carpenter marketing, it would be wise to evaluate how the cost compares to other carpentry business marketing ideas to see if it’s worth the expense for your business. In busy seasons, it might be extremely effective!

13. Reputation Management Affects Your Carpentry Business

Did you know 85% of people search online to find local carpentry providers? Those same people are also researching your reputation—what are your past customers saying about the quality of your work? Were your prices fair? Did you show up on time and do what you said you were going to do?

Offline, you can use word-of-mouth to your advantage. Ask your satisfied customers if they can think of any neighbors, friends, family, or acquaintances who might need your carpentry. Give them plenty of business cards to pass around, and ask if you can put a temporary sign in their yard with your business name and phone number.

Online, list your carpentry business on review websites and ask your happy customers to leave a review on Google. Doing so makes it more likely that you’ll get more carpentry leads.

14. Ad Retargeting Is a Fresh Carpentry Advertising Idea

Have you ever searched for a product online then noticed ads for it following you around the internet? That’s retargeting, and you can do it to help grow your carpentry business.

Retargeting is a great way to keep your carpentry business and carpentry website in front of potential customers who have already shown interest by visiting your website. This is one carpentry advertising idea well worth trying, especially if you’re already getting good traffic to your carpentry website. Throw in some SEO for carpenters for an extra visibility boost!

15. Make Your Carpentry Advertising Stand Out With a Unique Selling Point

If you want to stand out from your local competitors, your carpenter marketing needs a unique selling point. That could be your level of service, your experience in the industry, your competitive prices, your reputation, your employees, your guarantees—anything that sets you apart. Once you’ve figured out your USP, use that selling point in your carpenter marketing and carpentry advertising materials!

If you’re not sure where to start, take a look at how your competitors are generating carpentry leads. What’s their USP, and how can you beat it? What would matter more to your customers and the community you serve? Here are some ideas to get you started:

  • Eco-friendliness
  • Specialized services
  • Guarantees
  • Level of service

Can you think of something else that’s unique to your business? Maybe your origin story or how you got started? Use it!

16. Use Google’s Local Service Ads to Get More Carpentry Leads

Google’s Local Services ads are a type of pay-per-lead ad, and they appear at the top of the search results page. These ads get about 13.8% of clicks—that means if 50 people in your area search for carpentry providers, at least 7 of them will click one of those ads and turn into a carpentry lead.

How much more money would you make per month with 7 more carpentry contracts? For an extra boost, consider investing in carpentry digital marketing, then use SEO for carpenters and a professional carpentry website design to hook your potential customers.

17. Co-Market With a Relevant Business

Co-marketing means you’re teaming up with another business to share lead generation opportunities. For example, you might want to partner up with a property manager or complementary carpentry provider and offer a discount to customers who use both of your services. In the end, this kind of combined marketing can help grow your carpentry business since you’re both working to generate new carpentry leads.

18. Send Out Direct Mailers to Get More Carpentry Leads

Here’s another carpenter marketing idea that’s stood the test of time: direct mailers. Compared to the chaos that is most email inboxes today, old-school mailboxes are seeing a lot less action, which means your letter or postcard is much more likely to be seen and read by potential customers.

According to the Direct Marketing Association, direct mail has an average cost per lead of $51.40 for a general mailing list and $54.10 for postcards. Compare that to what you’re paying on average for carpentry leads from other sources to determine if this is a viable solution for you.

19. Canvass the Area to Generate Carpentry Leads

Canvassing an area to generate carpentry leads might seem like an old-school marketing tactic, but it’s a carpentry advertising idea worth trying. After all, consumers are more likely to work with you when they can put a face to your business. If you want to give canvassing a shot, try doing it after completing a carpentry job in the area.

Follow these tips for the best results:

  • Always be polite and courteous, even if you don’t get the reaction you want.
  • Set a goal for each interaction: set an appointment, get their contact information, or make a sale.
  • Have a script ready, so you’re better prepared.
  • Follow up with potential customers after getting their info.

When you’re prepared for a constructive interaction, you’re much more likely to get a favorable result. In this case, that’s a carpentry lead!

20. Increase Referrals to Get More Carpentry Leads

Referrals are a great way to capitalize on word-of-mouth marketing. Offer your existing customers an incentive to refer their friends, families and neighbors to your carpentry business, and give them a discount in return. Since people are far more likely to use a business that a friend recommends to them, this is one carpenter marketing and carpentry advertising idea you shouldn’t pass up.

21. Take Pictures of Completed Jobs

A picture can say a thousand words, so make sure you’re taking before and after photos of every carpentry job you take on. It’s a great way to show the quality of your work, and it gives you great content to post on your carpentry website and social media.

22. Use LinkedIn to Attract Carpentry Leads

LinkedIn is more than a great place to establish yourself as a carpentry expert and carpentry business owner—it’s a great place to generate carpentry leads, too. How? Do a search for local businesses and property managers you can connect with and talk about the carpentry you provide.

23. Hunt for Carpentry Leads on Craigslist

Craigslist might seem like a dated suggestion, but tons of people still use it when they need to find someone for a carpentry project. Take a quick scroll through the classifieds and see if anyone is looking for a carpentry provider. Write down their names and numbers, then get in touch with them to discuss the project they have in mind and schedule a consultation. More often than not, people searching for carpentry providers on Craigslist are ready to work with you now and pay for your services.

24. Send Out Press Releases About Your Carpentry Business

Press releases are a great way to share new and exciting developments about your carpentry business. Are you offering a new service? How about a discount for repeat customers or those who refer their neighbors? Write a press release that outlines these new items and send it off to your mailing list to start generating carpentry leads.

25. Don’t Pay for Carpentry Leads If You Don’t Need To

We’ve talked about using HomeAdvisor, Angi, Thumbtack and CraftJack for leads before, and all of them should be treated with caution. While they might deliver a lot of leads to your carpentry business, the quality of those leads often isn’t up to par. Whenever possible, you want to attract quality leads to your carpentry website that will turn into paying customers. All too often, lead generation services like HomeAdvisor and Angi just don’t deliver on quality. You’re likely to spend your time chasing down bad leads instead of talking to good ones.

A better alternative for helping grow your carpentry business is using carpentry digital marketing. Combined, SEO for carpenters and professional carpentry website design can increase your online visibility and win you more carpentry leads.
