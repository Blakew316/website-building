How to Respond to Your Google Reviews

JSBy Justin Scism·Jul 20, 2023·4 min read

As a business owner, we all know the power of online reviews. According to BrightLocal, 79% of consumers trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations, meaning the reviews on your Google My Business profile can have a big impact on you finding new customers. With so much focus on delivering great customer experiences, understanding how to best respond to Google reviews should be just as important in your overall strategy.

For a step-by-step guide to navigating your business profile in Google, see Google Business Profile Help. In this blog post, we will walk through key tips for building out effective review responses that can take your small business’s reputation to the next level!

Understand why Google reviews matter for your business

In today’s digital age, online reviews play a crucial role in a business’s reputation, and Google reviews are no exception. Customers often leave feedback about their experiences on Google, which can be read by potential customers seeking your services.

Whether it’s a restaurant, hotel, or retail store, having a high volume of positive reviews can boost your business’s credibility and attract new customers. On the flip side, negative reviews can have the opposite effect and turn potential customers away. By understanding the importance of Google reviews, businesses can take steps to improve their online reputation and establish themselves as reliable sources in their industry.

Respond quickly and appropriately to reviews, both positive and negative

Customer reviews are crucial to the success of any business. Responding quickly and appropriately to these reviews – whether they are positive or negative – is vital to building strong relationships with customers.

Positive reviews deserve a quick word of thanks, and it’s equally important to address negative reviews with a thoughtful and considerate response. By responding in a timely and professional manner, businesses can demonstrate their commitment to customer satisfaction and encourage repeat business.

In short, taking the time to respond to reviews can be an invaluable tool for building brand loyalty and maintaining a positive online reputation.

Don’t delete a negative review unless it violates Google’s terms of service

It can be tempting for companies to remove negative reviews in order to maintain a pristine image, but it’s important to proceed with caution. Unless the review violates Google’s terms of service, it’s best to leave it up. Why? Because negative reviews can actually be beneficial. They offer constructive criticism and an opportunity for the business to address areas of improvement.

When potential customers see a mix of both positive and negative reviews, they’re more likely to trust that the reviews are honest and genuine. So, embrace the negative reviews and use them to your advantage!

Take the time to answer customer questions in the comments section of the review

As a business, it’s important to always have your customers in mind. That’s why taking the time to address their questions and concerns in the comments section of reviews is crucial.

Not only does it show that you care about their feedback, but it also provides an opportunity to clarify any confusion and offer additional assistance. By actively engaging with your customers, you can strengthen the relationship and build a loyal following. It may take some extra effort, but the benefits of answering customer questions in reviews are well worth it in the end.

Show appreciation for positive reviews by thanking customers

When it comes to customer reviews, positive feedback is always a boost to morale and a sign that your business is headed in the right direction. As a business owner, it’s important to take the time to show your appreciation for those who take the time to leave a positive review.

A simple thank you can go a long way when it comes to building customer loyalty and strengthening your reputation. Remember, customers play a crucial role in the success of your business, so it’s important to let them know their voice is heard and valued. By thanking customers for their positive reviews, you’re not only showing your gratitude, but you’re also fostering a positive relationship with those who support your brand.

Offer appropriate solutions for customers who leave negative reviews

It’s never easy to see a negative review about your business. However, it’s important to remember that customers who take the time to leave feedback – even negative feedback – are invested in your product or service. Rather than ignoring negative reviews, it’s crucial to address the customer’s concerns and offer solutions.

There are several ways to do this, such as responding publicly to the review with an apology and an offer to discuss the issue further offline, or contacting the customer directly to remedy the situation. It may even be worthwhile to take the feedback to heart and make improvements to your product or service based on the customer’s concerns. Ultimately, responding to negative reviews in a timely and professional manner can not only improve the customer’s experience, but also demonstrate your commitment to providing quality service.
