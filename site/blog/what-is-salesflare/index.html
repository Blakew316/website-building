Why Use Salesflare? 5 Key Features & Benefits

Amid the many available CRM options, what makes Salesflare different? Here are five top features and benefits that make Salesflare a top pick among B2B businesses.

1. Automatic Sales Tracking

Taking control of your sales activity is much easier when you don’t have to remember every detail about your leads. Every Salesflare plan includes automatic sales tracking, with customizable dashboards that give you and your team a clear picture of your pipeline. Reminders to follow up, add notes, or reply to emails ensure you never miss an opportunity. Meanwhile, collaborative tools help your team stay aligned.

2. Built-In Email Integration

Salesflare integrates with Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, letting you send, track, and manage emails directly within the CRM or from your inbox. Instead of switching tabs, you can see relevant information next to your emails. If you want automated, personalized outreach at scale, Salesflare has you covered — and will even follow up until leads reply. Automated tracking of opens, clicks, and website visits give you the rich data you need to know what’s working and what’s not — so you can stay the course or pivot when needed.

3. Reporting Tools

Salesflare’s built-in dashboards let you track key performance metrics, such as revenue, deal stages, and team activity. You can create fully custom reports and dig into the data that matters most to your business. Easily forecast sales, track recurring revenue, and identify where leads are getting stuck in the funnel. It’s all about helping you make informed decisions based on real-time insights, so you can work smarter, not harder.

4. Mobile App

Salesflare’s mobile app lets you track calls, log notes, scan business cards, access client data, and follow up from anywhere. Whether you’re traveling, working from a coffee shop, or meeting with a client off-site, the mobile app allows you to find the information you need, when you need it. In fact, 100% of Salesflare’s features are accessible from the mobile app, so you always have what you need at your fingertips. Call histories also get logged within the app, while notifications alert you to email opens and replies, website visits, and other prospect activity.

5. Collaboration Made Simple

With Salesflare’s collaborative tools, everything is in one place. Everyone can view customer conversations to offer the best customer experience. Task hand-offs are also seamless, keeping deals moving and distributing the workload, while permissions settings let you decide what information to share or keep private. You’ll also love Salesflare’s “team relationships” capabilities. It lets you see your team’s connections at different companies, so you can focus on relationship-building with key prospects.
