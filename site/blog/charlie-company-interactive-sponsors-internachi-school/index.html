Charlie Company Media Sponsors InterNACHI School

DBBy Dave Balogh·Feb 2, 2017·1 min read

CHARLOTTE, NC. — February 2, 2017 – Charlie Company Media has officially been named a sponsor for InterNACHI’s school for new and seasoned home inspectors.

The InterNACHI School focuses on providing a place for new and seasoned home inspectors to hone their craft and continue learning and exploring the home inspection industry. Charlie Company Media’s goal in sponsoring the school is to help home inspectors further their education by educating them on digital marketing practices as well as online and social media trends.

Brian Dickerson, Director of Strategic Partnerships shared, “It means we’ll gain a deeper understanding of marketing needs and business needs of home inspectors so that we can help them grow and succeed in a competitive market place through our business solutions.”

InterNACHI operates in 65 different countries and in nine languages with members all over the world who participate in its inspection trainings and education programs. Accredited classes are offered online, and conducted at its location in Boulder, CO with industry leading professionals.

“We are excited for Charlie Company Media’s InterNACHI School sponsorship and look forward to the wealth of digital marketing knowledge Charlie Company can offer our members,” said Nick Gromicko, Founder of InterNACHI.

About Charlie Company Media

Charlie Company Media is a division of Charlie Company Holdings, Inc. one of the nation’s largest media companies. Charlie Company Media is a digital marketing and solutions organization that specializes in creating the total web presence that small businesses need to make sure they reach their online audience of current and prospective customers. When clients partner with Charlie Company Media, they choose a team of industry-leading experts who work with them to develop and execute a comprehensive total web presence plan. Currently, Charlie Company Media serves 10,000+ clients on a month-to-month basis.

About InterNACHI School

The InterNACHI School offers accredited inspection training at its Boulder, CO facility. InterNachi was founded in 1994 by Nick Gromicko, and currently has 17,000+ members worldwide.
