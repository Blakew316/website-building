8 Benefits of Using Looka Logo Maker

MEBy Meridian Editorial Team·Sep 10, 2024·5 min read

No matter how big or small your business is, having a logo helps you stand out, stick in your target audience’s mind, and convey a sense of professionalism. However, paying a graphic designer to create a logo that captures the spirit of your brand can get expensive.

Employing a freelance designer can cost anywhere between about $250 and $2500, with larger design agencies charging as much as $10,000 for a logo.

While small businesses need these services, they don’t always have the budget. It’s clear that there is a gap in the market; thankfully, Looka Logo Maker is here to help.

#1. Quick turnaround

You have a few options when you need a logo for your business. You can contract a designer or do it yourself. Both options have some drawbacks for business owners, such as time or money.

Looka’s AI-powered logo maker allows you to instantly build slick, professional logos in no time at all, meaning you can get back to doing what you do best, like growing your business or delivering quality service for your clients.

#2. User-friendly interface

Looka was built to take the pain out of creating a logo. The process is straightforward, and the interface is perfect for business owners without graphic design experience.

To get a tastefully designed logo, all you need to do is enter your business name, industry, and any preferences for style, colors, and symbols. From there, Looka will generate designs that suit your business. Once you’ve found a design that you like, you can edit and fine-tune the design until you get a logo that resonates with your brand.

Overall, the process is simple, intuitive, and suitable for people with varying degrees of experience and knowledge.

#3. Value for money

Starting a business comes with many overheads—and that’s before considering the cash flow you need to buffer you through the ups and downs. Sinking too much of your revenue into a logo is just not an option for many business owners.

Looka logo maker offers a good blend of quality and value for money, making it an ideal option for small businesses. The Looka pricing model provides a lot of flexibility, and you can get a logo that you love for as little as $20.

#4. Solid customization options

One of the most compelling selling points of Looka for small business owners is that designs are AI-generated. However, while automating the processes saves a lot of time and money, it’s good to have a little control over the final output. Thankfully, Looka has good customization options to help you get closer to your vision.

Some of the things you can change include layouts, colors, fonts, and symbols. Additionally, you can adjust sizes and spacing and even add new backgrounds.

#5. High-quality file formats

Any logo that you design with Looka Logo Maker comes in high-resolution PNG and JPEG file formats. You can also download your logo in SVG or EPS, which is perfect for printed materials. This is perfect if you plan to use a sublimation printing service that requires high-resolution images.

High-quality files look slick and professional and display well on screens, online product catalogs, printed materials, and even branded merchandise. Furthermore, you can also add these files to popular design tools if required.

#6. Brand kits

A brand kit is a set of digital assets that represent your brand's look and feel. It contains lots of things, like:

  • Logos
  • Letterhead
  • Social media profile
  • Flyers
  • Posters
  • Blog banners
  • And much, much more.

Essentially, it’s a comprehensive marketing toolkit that lets you brand all your communication with your new logo. With over 300 branded templates, you’ll easily find something that meets your needs, whether it’s for social media, business documents, or marketing materials. The tool even comes with web templates, allowing you to create a visually striking web presence in no time

#7. Solid customer support

Looka Logo Maker has lots of different support channels. You can reach out via chat, email, or by phone. What’s more, there are good customer self-service options, too, with a knowledge base stacked full of helpful articles that answer any query you can imagine.

Even though it’s a one-off purchase, the Premium Logo Package also comes with lifetime technical support. That is great news for small businesses that don’t need very extensive or comprehensive branding options but still need a high-quality logo that represents their brand for years to come.

#8. No risk

We’ve saved the biggest benefit for last. Looka has an innovative pricing model that allows you to “design, edit, preview, and save as many logos as you want for free.” Then, once you’re happy with the logo, you can buy the rights to use it for your business.

Essentially, this means that you can use the tool without making a financial commitment, which means there is no risk.

Looka's pricing model has something to suit everyone. You can grab a PNG file for a one-off fee of $20 under the Basic Logo Package. Alternatively, a $65 Premium Logo Package gets you a fully editable logo with a range of customization options.

However, if you’re looking for something more advanced, you can get a Brand Kit Subscription for just $96 each year, which gives you access to a wide range of branded assets for social media, letters, invoices, and more. If you also need an AI-generated website, the Brand Kit Website Subscription is $129 per annum.

Final thoughts

Looka logo maker is a competitively-priced, highly user-friendly tool that helps you build a logo for your brand in no time. This AI-driven app allows small business owners to generate and edit a wide variety of designs that take their branding game to a whole new level.

If you need a logo, a brand kit, or web design, check out Looka today.
